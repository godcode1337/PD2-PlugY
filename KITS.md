# Bosser kits (Season 13)

Custom cube recipes in the **testing** modpack that hand a fresh character a full endgame kit.
Launch with the **PD2 SP Testing** shortcut (`PlugY.exe -direct -txt`); the recipes do nothing in the normal launcher.

## How to use

Put in the Horadric Cube and transmute:

**1 Identify Scroll + 1 chipped gem (the build's colour) + N Town Portal Scrolls**, where **N = the part number**.

| Build | Gem | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Hydra Sorceress | Chipped **Ruby** | 1–9 |
| Lightning Fury Javazon | Chipped **Topaz** | 1–7 |
| Whirlwind Barbarian | Chipped **Diamond** | 1–7 |
| Wake of Fire Assassin | Chipped **Skull** | 1–7 |
| Poison Strike Necromancer | Chipped **Emerald** | 1–7 |
| Sacrifice (Lightning) Paladin | Chipped **Sapphire** | 1–5 |

- The scrolls and the gem are consumed. Buy scrolls from any town vendor. If a bought scroll lands in your Tome instead of your inventory, fill the Tome to 20 first.
- The gems must be single **chipped** gems. Duplicate one with the testing modpack's Key recipe if you're short.
- Runewords can't be created by the cube. Those parts give you the socketed white base plus the runes, and you socket them in the order shown.
- Every unique rolls random stats. Reroll with a Horadrim Orb (testing recipe) if you want better ones.

## Not in the kits: where to find them in the item pack stash

The shared stash comes from the repo's `Save\Library`. Page layout (from the README):

- **Skillers, life/resist small charms, other charms:** pages **120–139**. Lightning Fury wants lightning-damage large charms from here, not skillers.
- **Facets and other jewels:** pages **10–19** (magic and rare) and **30–39** (unique jewels such as Rainbow Facets).
- **Call to Arms and other runewords** already made: pages **100–119**.
- **Crafted items** (e.g. the WW guide's crafted Ogre Maul and Vampiric helm): the **Bases** character has every base item; use the testing crafting recipes.
- **Corruptions** (pierce for Lightning Fury, sockets for WW): put a Key plus a rune in the cube (see the README's modpack section).
- **Runes, gems, uber keys:** pages **1–9** (workstation).
